Paytable & Rules

Line pays are multiples of the line bet (total bet ÷ 10) and pay left to right on adjacent reels. Values below update with your current bet.

Rules

  • ⭐ Wild substitutes for every symbol except 💎 Scatter. A line pays only its highest win.
  • 💎 Scatter pays anywhere as a multiple of the total bet: 3 → ×3, 4 → ×15, 5 → ×100.
  • 3 or more Scatters award 10 free spins at the triggering bet. All free-spin wins are ×2. Free spins can retrigger (max 100 per bonus).
  • 10 fixed paylines, shown below. Wins on different lines are added.
  • Malfunction voids all pays and plays.

Provably Fair

Each spin's reel stops are HMAC-SHA256(serverSeed, clientSeed:nonce), split into 32-bit words and scaled onto each reel strip. The server seed hash is published before you play; rotate to reveal the seed and verify any past spin.
